Shoulder Workout



Exerises 1/10

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press


sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit upright on a bench with your back supported and hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder level.
  • Keep your feet flat on the floor and engage your core to maintain a stable posture.



Exerises 2/10

sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Adjust the seat height so the handles are at shoulder level.
  • Sit with your back firmly against the pad and place your feet flat on the floor.
