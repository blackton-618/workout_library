Bicep Workout



Exerises 1/10

Dumbbell Bicep Curls


sets: 4 sets

reps:8-10 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand upright with your feet shoulder-width apart and hold a dumbbell in each hand.
  • Keep your elbows close to your sides and curl the dumbbells upward.
  • Press upward, focusing on bicep contraction.



Exerises 2/10

Hammer Curls


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Curl the dumbbells upward toward your shoulders while keeping your palms facing inward.
  • Lower the dumbbells back down with control.
  • Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.

Exerises 5/10

Preacher Curls


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit at the preacher curl bench and place your upper arms firmly against the padded support.
  • Hold the barbell, EZ bar, or dumbbell with an underhand grip.
  • Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.



Exerises 6/10

Incline Dumbell Curls


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Set an incline bench to about 45–60 degrees and sit back with a dumbbell in each hand.
  • Let your arms hang straight down with your palms facing forward.
  • Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
