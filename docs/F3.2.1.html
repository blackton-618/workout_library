Cardio Workout



Exerises 1/10

Jumping Jacks


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-14 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with your feet together and your arms relaxed by your sides.
  • Jump up, spread your legs out to the sides, and raise your arms overhead.
  • Land softly with your feet together again and bring your arms back down.



Exerises 2/10

High Knees


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your arms relaxed by your sides.
  • Run in place while lifting one knee toward your chest at a time.
  • Keep your core tight and pump your arms naturally to maintain balance.



Exerises 3/10

Butt Kicks


sets: 4 sets

reps:8-16 reps

rest time:120sec

Est time:5-13min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand tall and begin jogging in place with a light bounce.
  • Kick your heels up toward your glutes one leg at a time.
  • Keep your chest up and stay on the balls of your feet throughout the movement.



Exerises 4/10

Mountain Climberes


sets: 3 sets

reps:12-16 reps

rest time:60sec

Est time:2-7min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Start in a high plank position with your hands under your shoulders.
  • Drive one knee toward your chest, then switch legs quickly.
  • Keep your body straight and your core braced the entire time.



Exerises 5/10

Burpees


sets: 2 sets

reps:10-14 reps

rest time:50sec

Est time:5-12min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms by your sides.
  • Squat down, place your hands on the floor, and jump your feet back into a plank.
  • Jump your feet forward again, stand up, and explosively jump upward at the top.



Exerises 6/10

Jump Rope


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-16 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:10-16min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with the rope behind you and hold one handle in each hand.
  • Swing the rope over your head and jump just high enough to let it pass underneath you.
  • Keep your elbows close and land softly with a slight bend in your knees.



Exerises 7/10

Jogging


sets: 2 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:60sec

Est time:2-8min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand tall and begin a light running motion in place.
  • Lift your knees slightly and pump your arms naturally to keep rhythm.
  • Keep your pace controlled and land softly on the midfoot.



Exerises 8/10

Cycling


sets: 3 sets

reps:12-15 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:6-14min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit upright with a slight forward lean and begin a pedaling motion with your legs.
  • Drive one leg down while the other lifts, keeping the motion smooth and controlled.
  • Keep your core engaged and your back tall throughout the ride.



Exerises 9/10

Stair Climbing


sets: 4 sets

reps:12-14 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-8min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand facing a flight of stairs or a stable step.
  • Step up with one foot, then bring the other foot up to maintain a steady rhythm.
  • Keep your chest lifted and drive through the whole foot as you climb.

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min

Instruction
