Exerises 2/10

Hand-Release Push-Ups


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Start in a plank position. Lower your entire body under control until you are completely flat on the floor.
  • Briefly lift both hands off the ground by squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  • Place your hands back down and explosively press your body back up in one rigid line.

Exerises 5/10

Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts


sets: 2 sets

reps:10-14 reps

rest time:50sec

Est time:5-12min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand tall holding dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  • Hinge at your hips and push them backward while keeping your back completely flat and a slight bend in your knees.
  • Lower the weights down the front of your legs until you feel a deep stretch in your hamstrings.
  • Squeeze your glutes to drive your hips forward and return to the starting position.

Exerises 7/10

Sit-Ups


sets: 2 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:60sec

Est time:2-8min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Cross your hands over your chest or place your fingers gently behind your ears.
  • Engage your core and lift your entire torso off the floor until your chest is close to your thighs. Slowly lower your body back down under control to the starting position.



Exerises 8/10

Battle Ropes


sets: 3 sets

reps:12-15 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:6-14min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, and hold one rope end in each hand.
  • Alternately whip your arms up and down as fast as possible to create continuous waves in the ropes. Keep your core tight and maintain a strong, stable stance throughout the timed set.



Exerises 9/10

Renegade Rows


sets: 4 sets

reps:12-14 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-8min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Start in a push-up plank position with your hands gripping two dumbbells flat on the floor.
  • Keep your feet spread wide for balance and your core locked tight. Row one dumbbell up to your hip while balancing on the other arm, lowering it under control before switching sides.



Exerises 10/10

Kettlebell Swings


sets: 2 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, and hold the kettlebell with both hands between your legs.
  • Snap your hips forward to swing the kettlebell up to chest height, leading with your elbows.
