Calisthenics Workout



Exercises 1/10

Hollow Body Hold



>

sets:3 sets

hold time:20-30 seconds

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Lie flat on your back. Press your lower back firmly into the floor so there’s no gap underneath.
  • Lift your legs, head, and shoulder blades slightly off the ground.
  • Extend your arms overhead.
  • Hold this banana-like shape.



Exercises 2/10

Leg raise



>

sets:3 sets

reps:8-12 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Grab a pull-up bar with an overhand grip.
  • Engage your shoulders (don't just hang passive).
  • Drive your knees up toward your chest using your abs—not momentum.
  • Lower them slowly under control without swinging.



Exercises 3/10

Plank Shoulder Taps



>

sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 taps per side

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Get into a high push-up position with your hands shoulder-width apart and feet slightly wider for balance.
  • Keep your hips completely still as you lift one hand to tap the opposite shoulder.
  • Alternate sides slowly.

Exercises 5/10

Seated Pike Compression Lifts



sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 lifts

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit with legs extended straight in front of you and hands on the floor next to your knees.
  • Lean forward slightly and lift both legs straight off the floor using pure core tension.



Exercises 6/10

Toes-to-Bar



sets:3 sets

reps:5-8 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Hang from a pull-up bar.
  • Drive your straight legs all the way up until your toes physically touch the bar between your hands, controlling the descent without using momentum.



Exercises 7/10

Bodyweight Rollouts



sets:3 sets

reps:8-12 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Kneel on the floor with an ab wheel or hands on sliding towels.
  • Keeping a solid core, roll forward as far as you can control, then pull back using your core strength.



Exercises 8/10

Hanging Windshield Wipers



sets:3 sets

reps:6-8 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Hang from a pull-up bar and bring your toes up toward the bar.
  • Rotating at your hips, sweep your legs side-to-side in a controlled arc using your obliques.



Exercises 9/10

L-Sit Hold



sets:3 sets

hold time:10-20 sec

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit on the floor or use parallettes.
  • Place your hands by your hips, press down hard to lift your hips and legs off the ground, holding your straight legs out horizontally at 90 degrees.



Exercises 10/10

Dragon flags



sets:3 sets

reps:8-10 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Lie flat on your back on a sturdy bench. Position your head right at the top edge of the bench.
  • Actively pull down on the bench with your hands as if trying to bend it.
  • This locks your upper back into the bench and creates a solid foundation.
