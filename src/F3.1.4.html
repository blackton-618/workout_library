Tricep Workout



Exerises 1/10

Close-Grip Bench Press


sets: 4 sets

reps:8-10 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Grip barbell shoulder-width apart.
  • Lower bar to mid-chest keeping elbows tucked.
  • Press upward, focusing on triceps contraction.



Exerises 2/10

Tricep Dips (Parallel Bars)

sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:60sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Keep torso upright, elbows close.
  • Lower until arms form 90°.
  • Push back without locking elbows.

sets:3 sets

reps:10-14 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Hold dumbbell overhead with both hands.
  • Lower behind head slowly.
  • Extend arms fully without flaring elbows..



Exerises 4/10

Skull Crushers

sets:3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:40sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Bend elbows to bring bar near forehead.
  • Extend arms back to start.

Instruction
