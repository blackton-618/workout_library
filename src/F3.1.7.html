Legs Workout



Exerises 1/10

Leg Press


sets: 4 sets

reps:8-10 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Sit comfortably on the leg press machine with your back and head supported.
  • Place your feet flat on the platform, hip-width apart.
  • Press the weight upward by extending your legs.

Exerises 10/10

Wall Sit


sets: 3 sets

reps:10-12 reps

rest time:90sec

Est time:5-10min



00 : 00


Instruction

  • Stand with your back against a flat wall.
  • Slide your butt down the wall until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
  • Keep your core engaged and hold the position.
